Introduction

Read the introduction.

When baking, the density of an ingredient can affect how much of it is needed to achieve the desired result. In this lab, you will practice determining density by measuring the mass and volume of biscuit ingredients. You will then use your measurements to calculate density and compare the densities of different ingredients. Remember that density describes how much mass is contained within a given volume, so both accurate mass and volume measurements are important. Use the materials and procedures below to complete this activity.

Biscuit ingredients

  • 169 g milk
  • 1 1/8 cup of flour
  • 8 1/2 tsp of shortening

Conversion factors you may need

1 cup = 16 tablespoons1 cup milk = 3.17 ounces
